Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

Your email, display name, and any profile info you choose to add (avatar, region, profession). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from them, nothing else.

No phone number required

PrivateChat never asks for your real phone number. Instead, you get an in-app number used only to find and add you as a contact.

Who can message you

Nobody can message you freely. A person must send a contact request first, with one introductory message, and you choose to accept or decline before a full conversation opens.

Your messages

Messages are stored securely to deliver them to you, including while you're offline. You can delete your own sent messages at any time. Group messages are visible to group members.

Online status & read receipts

You control whether others can see your online status and read receipts through Settings. Turning off read receipts also hides other people's read status from you.

Calls

Voice and video calls connect directly between you and the other person where possible. A relay service may be used to help the connection succeed on some networks, but it does not store your call content.

Data deletion

You can remove contacts, leave groups, and delete your own messages at any time from within the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through the Feedback option in Settings.
