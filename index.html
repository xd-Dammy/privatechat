How you start a conversation

  1. Share your in-app number

    When you sign up, you get a number that works only inside PrivateChat. Your real phone number is never requested.

  2. Someone sends one intro message

    A new person can send a single message with their request. They can't send more until you respond.

  3. You accept or decline

    Accept and the full conversation opens. Decline and it stays closed.

What's in the app

  • Voice and video calls

    Call your contacts directly from the chat.

  • Group chats

    Create a group, add members, and choose who manages it.

  • Status updates

    Post a photo that your contacts can see for 24 hours, and hide it from anyone you pick.

  • View-once media

    Send photos, videos, and voice notes that can be opened only once.

  • App lock

    Require a PIN, or Face ID or fingerprint, every time the app opens.

  • You control what others see

    Turn read receipts and online status on or off in Settings.

Start chatting on your terms

Sign up with email, Google, or Apple. It works in your browser, and you can add it to your home screen.
